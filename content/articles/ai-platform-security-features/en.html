Choosing the Right Fit

Selecting the “best” platform hinges on your unique business requirements, data sensitivity, and regulatory environment. Here’s how to wrap up your assessment process:

  1. Map Requirements to Features: Align each platform’s offerings - encryption, privacy controls, incident response - to the pillars established in Part 1.

  2. Request Clarity on Data Retention: Regardless of the provider, seek explicit details on data handling policies.

  3. Evaluate Scalability & Integration: Consider how each tool slots into existing CI/CD pipelines, data governance standards, and internal workflows.

  4. Perform a Risk Assessment: From a technical and legal standpoint, parse each TOS for liability clauses, breach notifications, and indemnification.
