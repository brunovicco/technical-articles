The other day, I discussed the taxonomy of memory (episodic, semantic, and procedural) and how each type requires a different infrastructure layer in a financial assistant.

Today, I want to discuss the problem that no storage layer solves on its own: what happens between the LLM generating a response and that response reaching the client.

That interval of milliseconds of processing is where an institution can commit a regulatory violation unintentionally, without malice, and with no possibility of reversing it after the message has been delivered.

The problem the legal archive does not solve

You can have S3 WORM configured in Compliance Mode, a Data Warehouse with every audit table, and Redis Checkpointer saving every conversation turn. All of that is indispensable, but it serves to prove what happened after the fact.

If the LLM delivers the phrase "invest now, do not miss this opportunity" to the client, the log will record that a violation occurred. That does not prevent the infraction. CVM Resolution 30 does not accept "the model generated it on its own" as a defense.

The LGPD vs. AML paradox (and an architectural way out)

With guardrails and a legal archive in place, the final tension emerges: the LGPD (Art. 18) guarantees data subjects the right to request deletion of their data. Brazil's Central Bank requires financial operations to be retained for up to 10 years for AML/CFT purposes.

Summary

Guardrails in investments are not output validation; they are automated regulatory control. The distinction matters because it changes how you design them: it is not about checking whether the response is good; it is about checking whether the response falls within the legal limits defined by the Central Bank and the CVM.

The architecture presented has three defense layers:

  • regex for obvious, immediate violations;

  • automatic injection for mandatory disclaimers;

  • DSPy for semantic violations beyond the reach of regex.

With a Circuit Breaker ensuring that the failure of one layer does not compromise the others.

And all of this needs to be recorded, because the legal archive that stores "guardrail triggered with violation 2 blocked" is just as important as the archive that stores the transaction. It is proof that the system was working.
